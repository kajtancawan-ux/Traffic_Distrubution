# Traffic_Distrubution
A code that can distribute traffic from many streets and not just one

# The Problem
## Traffic is extremely suffocating and compact in small or compact cities like Masbate city.
## This means that crossing busy streets, Going to school, or even just incase of a emergency it takes way too long to arrive or extremely dangerous.
## Also the fact that traffic can cause greenhouse gasses can pile up in a single area making it hard to breathe.

# Objectives
## I seek to make traffic less compact and make arriving to your destination faster.
## This goal also ensures less accidents or fatalities that may occur from exccessive traffic pile up.
